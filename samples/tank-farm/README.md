# Tank farm

Six storage tanks with level, temperature and vapour pressure, their outlet valves, and the transfer and loading pumps that move product between them. Level alarms are the whole point here - four per tank.

**129 tags** — 78 Bool, 2 Int, 49 Real

- Storage tanks
- Transfer
- Loading
