# Commercial coffee machine

A bean-to-cup coffee machine for a canteen or a hotel lobby: a coffee boiler and a steam boiler, two grinders, a brew unit, a refrigerated milk system with its pump, a chocolate powder doser, and the cleaning, descaling and drink counters a service technician reads.

**106 tags** — 60 Bool, 14 Int, 32 Real

## Areas

- Boilers and water
- Grinders and brew unit
- Milk system and powder
- Machine, cleaning and counters

## Import it

Tags → drop `tags.csv` on the upload panel, or click to browse.

The importer will report corrections on these, rather than applying
them silently:

```

1ST_SHOT_COUNT
```
